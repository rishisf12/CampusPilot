## Routes for the leads with no published email

Their site is the route: open the contact page and use their form, or call. For a site with no form
(Tier 1 + most of Tier 2) you are calling anyway — that is the point of the pitch.

Two more free routes to add them:
- **BBB business profile** → "Business Management" lists the owner/principal and employee count.
  Search the business name on bbb.org, filter by city.
- **State contractor licensing board** → the licence is issued to the *company*, with the individual
  "qualifier" named. Free on every state's site, and it proves they are a business, not a
  sole trader.

## Scoring for the calls you make

| Lead type | Ticket | Why they say yes |
|---|---|---|
| Site returning an error | $3,500–$5,000 | They are losing calls right now |
| Multi-location / commercial (SLM, Murray Hill) | $4,000–$9,000 | Every location needs its own page and its own reviews |
| Single-location with no form | $1,800–$3,000 | Cheapest fix, fastest close, easiest first reference |
| Care plan on top | $400–$800/mo | Hosting, edits, new pages, reporting |

Close every deal on: **50% up front, their domain and hosting in their name, live in 7 days,
30 days of free fixes after launch.** Then ask for the one thing that makes this repeatable —
*"who else in your trade is dealing with this?"*
