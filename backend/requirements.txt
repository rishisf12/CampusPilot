# --- Observability: scan queue ----------------------------------------------
# RQ is BSD-3-Clause and deliberately small (~2k lines). A scan is a linear,
# expensive, on-request job; BullMQ would mean running a Node service purely as a
# queue and Celery would bring a result backend and beat scheduler we never use.
# Concurrency is pinned to 1 so scans cannot overload a small VPS.
rq==1.16.2
redis==5.2.1
