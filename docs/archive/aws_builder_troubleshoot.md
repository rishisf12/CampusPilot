# AWS Builder Center "Account Does Not Exist" Troubleshooting

This is actually a VERY common issue. Here are the exact fixes:

## 1. CHECK YOUR EMAIL
- Did you use a .edu email? Builder Center requires verified .edu addresses
- Check spam/junk folder for the verification email
- The email must end with @iiitdmj.ac.in OR be a verified .edu address

## 2. STUDENT VERIFICATION (SheerID)
- AWS Builder Center uses SheerID to verify student status
- Verification can take 24-48 hours
- If it's pending, your account may show "does not exist" until approved
- You may need to re-submit docs: class schedule, student ID, enrollment proof

## 3. ACCOUNT CREATION ISSUES
- You may have started sign-up but not completed it
- Try the full flow again: builder.aws.com → Sign Up
- Use your IIITDMJ email: @iiitdmj.ac.in

## 4. TRY DIFFERENT PAGES
- builder.aws.com/student-rewards
- builder.aws.com/learn/students
- Try both .com and regional endpoints

## 5. WHAT TO DO NOW

### Option A: Re-verify Student Status
1. Go to builder.aws.com
2. Click "Sign In" → "Forgot password" if needed
3. Use your IIITDMJ email
4. Complete SheerID verification again
5. Wait for confirmation email

### Option B: Contact AWS Support
- Go to: console.aws.amazon.com/support
- Create a "billing/catalog" case
- Explain: "Builder Center account shows 'does not exist' after sign-up"
- Mention: IIITDMJ student, .edu email used

### Option C: Use AWS Free Tier Directly
- Go to: aws.amazon.com/free/
- Sign up with same email (no student verification needed)
- Get $100 credits immediately (no $20 bedrock bonus though)
- Still works with OpenCode + Bedrock

## 6. QUICK TEST
Try signing into the AWS Console (not Builder Center) with your email.
- If that works → Your AWS account exists, just Builder Center issue
- If that doesn't → Create new AWS account at aws.amazon.com

## ✅ BOTTOM LINE
Your AWS account likely exists, but Builder Center verification may be pending. 
Try the AWS Free Tier directly - you still get $100 credits and it works with OpenCode!
